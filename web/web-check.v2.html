Frequently Asked Questions

Does SpamWarden send my data to external servers?
No. The core engine is entirely client-side/local, meaning the data never leaves your environment during the initial analysis phase, ensuring strict privacy and compliance.
What does "PII Masking" mean?
Personally Identifiable Information (PII) masking automatically detects sensitive data like phone numbers, emails, or credit card patterns and replaces them with placeholders before processing or logging the payload.
How fast is the detection engine?
Extremely fast. You can use the "Performance Benchmark" button above to test your specific environment. It typically executes in under a millisecond per payload.
Can I customize the rules?
Yes, the ML model works alongside a configurable ruleset. You can train the vocabulary or adjust thresholds based on the specific type of spam or abuse targeting your platform.
